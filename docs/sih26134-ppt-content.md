## 📽️ SLIDE 2: Problem & Proposed Solution

### Header Banner:
> *Closed-loop skilling alignment for Maharashtra: sensing industrial demand, detecting skill gaps, and synthesizing 30-hour agile micro-modules.*

### Left Box — PROBLEM:
- Maharashtra faces an acute paradox: high youth underemployment alongside severe industrial talent shortages across Chakan EV, Aurangabad Pharma, and Nagpur Logistics.
- **Key Bottlenecks:**
  1. **3–5 Year Curriculum Revision Latency** vs. 6-month industry assembly line mutation.
  2. **Uniform One-Size-Fits-All Curricula** failing regional industrial clusters (Pune EV vs. Vidarbha Logistics).
  3. **75%+ MSME Informal Hiring** completely uncaptured by traditional job portals.

### Middle Box — OUR IDEA:
- **KaushalSetu:** An AI-driven closed-loop intelligence engine that bridges industrial demand with state vocational skilling through live multi-source telemetry, semantic graph alignment, and autonomous micro-curriculum synthesis.
- **Core Triad:**  
  `Demand Triangulation + BGE Semantic Matching (94.2% Recall@5) + Curriculum-Delta-Diff + 36-District GIS Twin`

### Right Box — Proposed Solution:
- • **Triangulated Demand Sensing** (Job Portals + MIDC CapEx + MSME Voice)
- • **BGE-Large dense semantic embeddings** & 1,200-node Skill DAG
- • Measured **94.2% Recall@5** verified on industrial vacancy corpora
- • **Autonomous Curriculum-Delta-Diff** (30-Hr modular bridge courses)
- • **36-District GIS Labor Digital Twin** & "What-If" Policy Simulator
- • **Marathi-First AI Voice Rojgar Sahayak** for rural candidates
- • **Verifiable Kaushal Passport** with DigiLocker QR integration
- • **Closed-loop employment retention telemetry** (EPFO / NAPS signals)
